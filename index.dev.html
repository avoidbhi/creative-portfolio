Chapter 08 / How I work

The method is research. The output is a line.

  1. 01

    Mine the comments

    Reddit threads, one‑star reviews, YouTube replies. The audience has already written the objections and the desires.

  2. 02

    Study the winners

    50+ Cannes and Clio winners, reverse‑engineered. Which structures survive a change of medium?

  3. 03

    Write a hundred

    100–200 concepts per brief, from a research system built on Claude and Lovable. The client sees three.

  4. 04

    Write for the ear and the thumb

    A line that can’t survive being said aloud, or scrolled past, isn’t finished.
