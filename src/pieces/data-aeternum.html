Your data, made tangible.

Data Aeternum is my BFA thesis: a data-privacy guide for consumers. The question it starts from: how much of our lives is stored online — and who can see it?

The answer is delivered in three parts: a website, an interactive handbook for understanding your personal data, and an NFC-enabled version of the handbook for print.

It began with a simple question — “What is being collected about you?” — and became a guide, for people, to their own data.

Live at data-aeternum.com
