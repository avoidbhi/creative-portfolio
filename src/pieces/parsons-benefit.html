A save-the-date, to 100,000 people.

I designed the save-the-date card for the Parsons Benefit in 2019. The card carries an animation — the Parsons logo moves as the card is held and tilted.

It was a fun brief: how do you make a static card feel alive, at the scale of a mailing list of 100,000+ people? That’s how many cards went out.
