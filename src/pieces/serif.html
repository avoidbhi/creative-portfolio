A logo that changes to fit infinite identities.

Serif is an organization dedicated to supporting the LGBTQIA+ community. The brief was to rebrand the whole system — logo, website, app, and social media — with a new identity that could carry a community forward.

It isn’t just a logo: it’s a complete identity, from the website down to print — new typography, new colour, a new logo, and a system built to stay with the community as it grows.

The two devices

The new logo is built on two devices. The first is the logo itself, which transforms into fluid, expressive shapes that reflect the infinite identities of the community. The second is a set of auras — fields of colour that sit around the logo — plus a dot-of-i device that can be placed anywhere the identity appears.
