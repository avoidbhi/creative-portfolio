“We are Cleanliness Paglus.”

The identity, in full — slide 24 of the 74-page social pitch. Not a product line, not a benefit claim: a name the brand can speak with. The detergent stops being sold; the house gets cleaned by someone who is in love with the job.

Footline under the identity: “Cleaning is our love language. Every stain has a story.”

The whole chapter is built on this voice — warm, over-caring, playfully obsessive — because a voice survives the product changing from powder to pod to liquid.
