Three questions about our data.

Three questions, in the first year of the thesis: How much data is there about you? How did it get there? What’s actually being done with your data?

To answer them I built a demo of a checkout process with a dark UX — a purchase flow that quietly harvested as much personal data as it could.

For the final presentation I compiled 40 GB of data — more than 15,000 files — into a 40-page dossier. It was printed, bound, and handed out.

There were two more pieces. A data receipt: every time you bought something, the site printed a receipt showing exactly what it had collected about you. And a plexiglass etch of the data-collection website’s code — the collection, made physical.
