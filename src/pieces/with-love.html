A gift card for the long distance.

With Love is a gift card for the traveling millennial — for the people who are always moving, always across time zones, always a little far away. The concept: a card that stretches, like the relationship does — a rubber-band loop of connection over the miles.

The whole system plays on that stretching loop: the card, the packaging, the web experience — the distance is part of the design, not a flaw in it.
