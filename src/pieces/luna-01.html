“To me, that is the story of being a woman.”

Founder-led brand storytelling email — the full letter, from Khushi, founder of LUNA by Khush.

The letter

They say the lotus is one of the most beautiful flowers in the world. But for a long time, I was more mesmerised by where it comes from than how it looks. The lotus roots itself in the mud. It grows in the dark, through the dirtiest, heaviest waters. Yet somehow, it breaks the surface and flourishes — pure, untouched, and radiant.

Life isn’t always clear water. We all face struggles that pull at us in seasons of darkness. But just like the lotus, you have this incredible, sacred ability to regenerate. Every single day you wake up, you are that flower breaking the surface.

I founded LUNA by Khush because I wanted to create more than just makeup. I wanted to create a reminder to be on our feminine side. The “LLUUNNAA” in our name represents enlightenment in that darkness — the glow that stays with you even when the sun goes down.

When you wear our products, I don’t want you to feel like you’re “putting on a face.” I want that sparkle on your skin to be a reflection of the fire already burning inside you.

Khushi N Kotecha, Founder · LUNA by Khush
