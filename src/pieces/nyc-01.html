“Build the skills, ship the proof.”

Not Your College (NYC) — v1. A website copy treatment for a bootcamp whose honest claim is that it is not a college and never pretended to be one.

The problem the page opens with

Four years of “Y”s — Y1: you learned the theory. Y2: you built assignments. Y3: you watched seniors get placed while you learned about resumes. Y4: you graduated with a degree and no proof of work. The page names the year the market starts asking a different question: what have you actually built?

The three answers

  • A structured path, not a playlistThe curriculum is sequenced — data → backend → frontend → full-stack projects, each week on purpose.
  • You build real thingsTaskFlow and QueryAI — the two capstones students ship, with the stack named on the page.
  • People, plus accountabilityLive sessions, doubt desks, and a community that keeps you moving when the playlist wouldn’t.

The honest comparison

Against YouTube (free but directionless), against degree programs (four years, no proof), against other bootcamps (price and outcome honesty) — the page argues the comparison out openly instead of hiding from it. Fee: ₹8,249, named once, plainly.
